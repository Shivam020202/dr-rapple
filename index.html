Discover Your Skin Type

Click on any skin type to learn more about its characteristics and how to care for it.

Dry Skin

Feels tight, may have flaky patches, and lacks natural moisture.

Oily Skin

Appears shiny, feels oily, and has enlarged, visible pores.

Combination Skin

Oily in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) and dry on the cheeks.

Normal Skin

Balanced, with no excessive oiliness or dryness. It's often clear and radiant.

Sensitive Skin

Reacts negatively to products, becomes red or inflamed easily, and feels irritated.

Acne-Prone Skin

Experiences frequent breakouts, pimples, and clogged pores.
