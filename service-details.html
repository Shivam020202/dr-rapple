About Chemical Peels

Chemical peels are professional skin treatments that use carefully formulated solutions to exfoliate and rejuvenate your skin, revealing a fresher, more youthful complexion.

Chemical peels work by applying a solution to the skin that causes controlled exfoliation and peeling. The depth of the peel can be customized based on your skin concerns and desired results.

How They Work

The chemical solution removes damaged outer layers of skin, stimulating cell turnover and collagen production. This reveals healthier skin underneath and improves texture and tone.

Treatment Levels

Light peels target surface concerns, medium peels address deeper imperfections, and deep peels provide dramatic results for significant sun damage or wrinkles.

Ideal Candidates

Those with acne scars, sun damage, fine lines, uneven skin tone, or rough texture. A consultation determines the best peel for your skin type and concerns.

Recovery Process

Light peels require minimal downtime (1-3 days), medium peels need about 1 week, while deep peels may require 2 weeks of recovery with specific aftercare.

Chemical Peel FAQs

Get answers to common questions about our professional chemical peel treatments

How often should I get a chemical peel?

Light peels can be done every 2-4 weeks, medium peels every 3-6 months, and deep peels are typically a one-time treatment. Your dermatologist will recommend a schedule based on your skin type and concerns.

Does a chemical peel hurt?

Light peels cause minimal discomfort (like mild tingling). Medium peels may sting temporarily. Deep peels require anesthesia. We use cooling techniques to maximize your comfort during all treatments.

Can chemical peels treat acne?

Yes! Salicylic acid peels are excellent for acne as they penetrate oil glands. Glycolic acid peels also help by exfoliating dead skin cells that clog pores. We customize the peel to your acne type.

How should I prepare for a peel?

Stop retinoids 3-7 days prior, avoid sun exposure, and don't wax or shave 24 hours before. For deeper peels, we may prescribe a pre-treatment skincare regimen to prepare your skin.

Can I wear makeup after treatment?

After light peels, you can typically wear makeup the next day. Medium peels require 5-7 days before makeup. We'll provide specific aftercare instructions and recommend mineral-based makeup when you're ready.

Are chemical peels safe for dark skin?

Yes, when performed properly. We use special formulations for melanin-rich skin to minimize pigmentation risks. Lactic acid and mandelic acid peels are particularly gentle options for darker skin tones.
